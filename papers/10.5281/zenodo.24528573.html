Rank-Preserving Fixed-Parameter Approximation for Factor Analysis with Disjoint Blocks or Two Overlapping Noise GroupsPreprint

Jiangze Zhu

2026

Abstract

We study approximation of a rational symmetric matrix by a positive semidefinite signal of prescribed rank at most r and positive semidefinite noise, under full Frobenius loss. We give deterministic (1 + ε) approximation algorithms for two support structures: arbitrarily many prescribed disjoint noise blocks, and exactly two prescribed, possibly overlapping noise groups covering all coordinates. Both have explicit fixed-parameter bit complexity in r and 1/ε, with an absolute polynomial input exponent. Thus the rank and accuracy dependence is separated from the input exponent while preserving rank, positivity, support and a purely relative guarantee. For disjoint blocks, a bounded support search and rational Krylov spaces locate a signal and compatible noises. A locally chosen quadratic-statistic certificate, using a finite-algebra trace formula, retains the full objective and permits joint algebraic sampling. For two groups, an aggregate characterization reduces the problem to a rank-constrained cross block. Convex completion within a left column space, rank-sensitive gradient selection, and a weighted covering of nearby column spaces guide a finite search. The latter algorithm also uses polynomial working space in the input, rank and inverse accuracy. Both algorithms treat zero error exactly. Positive-error block outputs have rational Gram factors, exact block outputs share one real-algebraic representation, and two-group outputs are rational covariance matrices.
