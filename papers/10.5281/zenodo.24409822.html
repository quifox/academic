Finite-Horizon Affinity Bounds for Visible-State Simulation of Hidden Markov SourcesPreprint

Jiangze Zhu

2026

Abstract

We study finite-memory generation of a known hidden Markov source with positive transitions. The simulator's state is determined by its outputs. Average quantization of predictive block laws gives finite-horizon error bounds: a converse for every admissible simulator and a recursive construction with a constant-factor state increase. At fixed total-variation tolerance, the stationary distortion threshold has inverse-horizon scale up to source-dependent constants. Positive examples show that one-symbol lower bounds can underestimate the leading bit coefficient by an arbitrarily large factor, and that a fixed perturbation can change the memory growth order. We specify the comparison constants.
